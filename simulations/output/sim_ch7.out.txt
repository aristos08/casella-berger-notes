7.2.4 WITH FREE VARIANCES THE LIKELIHOOD IS UNBOUNDED: no MLE exists
========================================================================
  component 1 pinned at the single observation x1=2.4544; component 2 held at (mu=1.061, var=1.0)
     v1        |  log-likelihood  |  predicted: const - 0.5*log(v1)
   1e-8         |     -1271.637    |     -1271.637
   1e-16        |     -1262.426    |     -1262.426
   1e-30        |     -1246.308    |     -1246.308
   1e-60        |     -1211.770    |     -1211.770
   1e-120       |     -1142.692    |     -1142.692
   1e-240       |     -1004.537    |     -1004.537
  -> the log-likelihood grows like -0.5*log(v1) without bound: the supremum is +infinity,
     and the simulated values track the predicted slope exactly.
     The unrestricted normal-mixture MLE does not exist; in practice one bounds the
     variances away from 0, penalizes them, or puts a prior on them.
